intersex DJ, producer, and designer. currently serving third places across LA, developing a sound rooted in alternative productions, Queer DIY subcultures, and gender euphoria. Her sets come in many formats from vinyl to live hardware, but each a cohesive attempt to channel raw emotional reflections of identity, vice, and virture through the transformative energy of the dance floor and community spaces.


Eva also curates and archives at EVAMM, a Queer music collective and online radio platform
